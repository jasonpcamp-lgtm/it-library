Queueing theory (Kingman's formula, 1961): wait time grows roughly as ρ / (1 − ρ). Past ~80% utilization, delay rises faster than the work added. Popularized for software by Tom DeMarco, Slack (2001) and Donald Reinertsen, Principles of Product Development Flow (2009).
