Priority Level Definitions
1
Priority 1 — Critical
Patient safety risk or total outage. Immediate response required.
2
Priority 2 — High
Significant operational impact. Urgent resolution needed.
3
Priority 3 — Moderate
Partial impact. Scheduled response appropriate.
4
Priority 4 — Low
Minimal impact. Standard queue handling.
5
Priority 5 — Planning
No current disruption. Informational or future work.
All incidents begin at Step 1. Any clinical system, device, or care delivery failure that places a patient at risk is auto-escalated to Priority 1 — no further scoring required. Non-safety incidents proceed to Step 2 and are classified by the intersection of Impact (breadth of users affected, availability of workarounds, and productivity loss) and Urgency (rate of damage over time and business or financial sensitivity). Priority is reviewable by the assigning technician with supervisor approval.
