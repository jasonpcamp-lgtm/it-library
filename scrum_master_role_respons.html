Scrum Master Roles & Responsibilities

The Scrum Master helps deliver against team commitments for projects, enhancements, incidents, and stories. This is done by acting as a servant leader, removing obstacles, pushing efficiencies, and driving continuous improvement. The Scrum Master helps the team deliver high-value work and products.

CORE QUESTIONS
Asks these questions to all team members, including themselves
01

Work Stagnation

"Where is work sitting, not being worked on?"

Identify queues, handoff points, and ownership gaps. Work that is “waiting” is usually the fastest path to improvement.

02

Non-Working Time

"How long are cards sitting in a non-working state?"

Track how long work spends paused, pending, or waiting. Time not spent progressing work is where flow breaks down.

03

Board Visibility

"Where can you see the work not being done on the board?"

Ensure the board tells the truth. Hidden work creates surprise, risk, and missed commitments.

04

Impediment Removal

"How can you remove impediments?"

Remove friction: access, approvals, environment issues, unclear requirements, and dependency delays.

05

Blocked Work

"What is marked Blocked?"

Blocked work should never be invisible. Make it explicit, time-box follow-ups, and assign a next action.

06

Lead Time

"What is the average lead time once a card is 'started'?"

Lead time reveals capacity, workflow health, and predictability. Use it to set expectations and improve flow.

07

Throughput

"Do you know team throughput?"

Throughput is the team’s delivery reality. Use it to guide planning, not hope or pressure.

08

Demo Readiness

"What can we demo soon and often?"

Demonstrations validate progress, reduce rework, and build trust. Optimize for small, frequent proof of value.

09

Stop Doing

"What are doing that we shouldn’t?"

Reduce waste: unnecessary meetings, unclear intake, rework loops, and “busy work” that doesn’t move outcomes.

RESPONSIBILITIES
Performs these tasks
01

PMO Collaboration

"How does the team align to portfolio governance?"
  • Collaborates with the PMO
  • Enters demands
  • Participates in PMO activities
02

Access & Operational Enablement

"What does the team need to execute without delay?"
  • Assists with support access
  • Shields the team — acts as interrupt buffer and triage
03

Quality in Delivery

"Is the work defined, testable, and documented inside the story?"
  • Develops strategies for testing and helps document within the story
  • Performs acceptance criteria discovery and input
  • Converts work to the correct ticket type
04

Blocker Removal

"What is stopping progress and who can move it?"
  • Assists the Product Owner with removing blockers
05

Agile Ways of Working

"Are we following the process we agreed to—without rigidity?"
  • Promotes agile principles, ways of working, and adherence to the process
06

Cadence & Ceremonies

"Is the team meeting rhythm driving execution and learning?"
  • Conducts daily scrums
  • Facilitates and manages the flow of ceremonies, including sprint planning and retros
  • Schedules ad hoc retros
07

Customer Engagement

"Are we validating value with the customer early and often?"
  • Collaborates with customers for demos
  • Obtains UAT signoff from the catalog form
08

Backlog Refinement

"Is the backlog ready, prioritized, and understandable?"
  • Refines product backlog with the Product Owner
09

Daily Progress Monitoring

"What do the metrics say today—and what action follows?"
  • Monitors progress by reviewing burn down charts and metrics on a daily basis
10

Production Change Coordination

"Are releases controlled, communicated, and successful?"
  • Coordinates change requests for pushes to PROD
